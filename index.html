How it's built

Coastline Golf

  • three.js: custom GLSL water (wave slopes, fresnel, sun glints, shoreline foam), gradient sky with clouds, stars and moon, instanced wind-swayed dune grass and cypress, bloom, ACES tone mapping, SMAA/FXAA and adaptive quality.
  • Open-Meteo live weather for Morro Bay: real wind pushes your ball, visibility thickens the marine layer, cloud cover greys the sky.
  • The real sun: solar position for Morro Rock at the current California time sets the light, from morning fog to golden hour to moonlight.
  • Real-world mapping
  • Custom physics: drag, backspin lift, wind drift, bounces, rolling friction per surface, sloped greens, lip-outs, water hazard drops.
  • Synthesised audio: surf, gulls, the strike and the cup rattle are all generated with WebAudio. No audio files.

Try ?time=sunset, ?time=morning&weather=fog or ?time=night.
